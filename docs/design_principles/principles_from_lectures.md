# Application of People-Centered Design Principles

## Context

Following client feedback, we pivoted from a standalone research portal to integrating research directly into the existing APSS website. We reapplied key PCD principles during this iteration.

**"Research"** tab is added to the main navigation for discoverability.   

**Levels of Processing:**
  * *Visceral:* Shifted to darker tones and removing peach to create a professional aesthetic
  * *Behavioral:* Preserved whitespace to reduce load and enhance readability
  * *Reflective:* Merged research into the main site to reinforce brand trust and consistency

* **Fast Feedback Cycle (FFC):** Iterated rapidly after client feedback—moving from *Observe* (critique) to *Frame* (redesigning the main site), *Brainstorm* (new features), and *Build* (revised sitemap & prototype).

* **Divergent to Convergent Thinking:** Explored multiple layout ideas for embedding research and student placements before converging on a streamlined, updated Sitemap.

* **Prototyping Fidelities:** Used quick Lo-Fi sketches to map out navigation flows, then updated our **Hi-Fi Figma prototype** to demonstrate the refined color scheme, integrated research layout, and functional exit button.

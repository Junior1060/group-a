# Usability Results Sheet (team copy)

One sheet per participant. The participant fills in the Quick Test form. You watch, and fill in this one.

**How to run it.** Hand them the Quick Test form. Read each task aloud. Do not point at the screen or say the menu words. Write down what they click and what they say. If they are stuck for a minute, help them and mark "Help". Collect their form at the end and staple it to this sheet.

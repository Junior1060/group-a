# Insights / Needs Chart: APSS Research Website

Each row is a theme from our affinity diagram (Content, Navigation, Visuals), with evidence from our four empathy maps of Erin Hidlebaugh, APSS Program Coordinator. A condensed version was presented on slide 5 at the October 7 customer check-in.

**Sources:** [affinity diagram](../affinity_diagrams/AFFINITY.png), [empathy maps 1 to 4](../empathy_maps/), [check-in slides](../../docs/pitch1/Customer-Checkin-1-Slides.pdf)

| Theme | Example from our research | What visitors need | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Content: show the research | Erin wants projects, a timeline, journal entries and community reports online. "Publish research" is a gain in two empathy maps. | Browse current and completed projects, open articles and posters, follow updates | APSS research exists but has no home online, so partners, funders and researchers cannot find or cite it |
| Content: plain language | All four empathy maps list the same pain: the current site is "too scientific", "too much text", "boring". | Short overviews written for the public, with detail one click deeper | Erin describes the audience as "average people, no scientists". Dense text drives them away |
| Content: impact | Affinity notes: "keep the impact statistics/numbers", "keep the impact numbers visually noticeable". | See what the research changed, in a few clear figures | Numbers make the work credible to funders and keep casual visitors reading |
| Navigation: quick paths | Affinity notes: "one-click access to support", "report a needle easily". Erin "wishes for people to access information quickly". | Reach any project, publication or partner site in one or two clicks | Erin calls the old site "too confusing". A short menu and clear links are the fix |
| Navigation: connected sites | Empathy map 3: "a link to redirect to the new site would be good". The project brief places the site beside the main APSS site and reportneedles.ca. | Move between the research site, the main APSS site and reportneedles.ca without getting lost | Three sites serve one mission. Visitors should never feel they have left APSS |
| Visuals: authentic and colourful | Empathy maps 1, 2 and 4: "no AI pictures", "really wants colours, good visuals", likes the Eaton Lab website. Affinity notes: "use real APSS photos", "use consistent image style". | Real photos of staff, events and the building, in a colourful, consistent style | Erin rejects AI images outright. Consent and authenticity are project constraints, and Eaton Lab is the benchmark she named |

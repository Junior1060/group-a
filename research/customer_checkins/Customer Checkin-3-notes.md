### Emergency Exit Button (Safety & Privacy Feature)
**Purpose:** Designed to protect user privacy, especially for individuals seeking sensitive information (e.g. how to get tested) who need to conceal their browsing activity quickly.  

**Functionality:** Clicking the button instantly redirects the user away from the site to a neutral third-party page (e.g. a local weather website or the Google homepage).    

**Visibility & Accessibility:** The button must be prominently displayed, easy to reach, and persistently available across all pages of the website.

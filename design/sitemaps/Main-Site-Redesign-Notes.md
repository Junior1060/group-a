# Main APSS Website: Proposed Sitemap

**Optional challenge from the October 21, 2026 check-in.** Redesign the main APSS website, with Research as a menu item that takes visitors to the new research website. This document records what we found on the live site and the structure we propose.

**Live site reviewed:** aidsprogramssouthsask.com, October 21, 2026.

**Diagrams:** [current structure](sitemap-main-site-current.png) and [proposed structure](sitemap-main-site-redesign.png).

## What we found on the current site

| Finding | Evidence | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research is hard to find | Research is the seventh and last item under About Us. The page is one paragraph about one project, with no papers or posters. | The new research site needs a front door that people can see. |
| Report a Needle is buried | reportneedles.ca is linked only in the body of the Needle Exchange and About Us pages. It is not in the menu or footer. | Reporting a needle is a top community task (affinity diagram: "report a needle easily"). |
| Services are split across menus | Naloxone Training sits under Education in the menu but under Our Services in the footer. | Visitors looking for help may not find it under "Education". |
| Long menus | 7 top-level items plus Donate. Education alone has 9 sub-pages. | Matches the affinity note that the menus feel "frightening and distracting". |
| Key details are hidden | Thursday nurse testing is only on the Get Help page. Hours are only in the footer. The address links to nothing. | The people who need services most need hours and location first. |
| No news, empty events | The Events page says "No Events Scheduled Right Now". There is no news section anywhere. | The affinity diagram asks for news and events on the home page. |
| Home page does not link | The hero and the three program cards are images that cannot be clicked. | Matches the affinity note that the icons "are not buttons". |
| Broken links | The address and two social icons link to "#". The Instagram handle has a typo. | Small issues, but they cost trust. |
| Reports are buried | Annual reports 2018 to 2024 sit at the bottom of the About Us page. | Funders and partners look for these. |

## Proposed structure

**Main menu, five items plus a Donate button:** Get Help, Learn, Research ↗, Get Involved, About Us.

- **Get Help comes first.** Erin told us most visitors are community members looking for services. Every service lives here, including Naloxone and Report a Needle.
- **Learn replaces Education.** Two of our team sitemaps already used this label. FAQs move here and to the footer.
- **Research ↗ opens the research website.** This is the challenge we were given. The ↗ marker tells visitors they are going to a linked site, the same signifier the research site uses for links back.
- **Get Involved gathers every way to help:** Volunteer, Events & News, Donate, Careers and Student Placements.
- **About Us keeps the organisation pages** and brings the annual reports forward.

**Top bar on every page:** phone number, today's hours, Contact Us, and a language switcher for English, French, Spanish and Portuguese (Erin, October 7).

**Footer on every page:** contact and hours, FAQs, Report a Needle ↗, Research site ↗, privacy, land acknowledgement.

## Why: links to our research

| Decision | Source |
| --- | --- |
| One-click access to the main services | Affinity diagram: "one-click access to support", "report a needle easily", "volunteer easily" |
| Fewer, shorter menus | Affinity diagram: navigation "can seem frightening and distracting" |
| FAQs and Contact out of the main menu | Affinity diagram: "FAQ + Contact in footer" |
| News and events on the home page | Affinity diagram: "show news/events on the Home page" |
| Two separate sites, linked both ways | Empathy map 5: Erin feels two separate websites work best for APSS |
| Home page that looks like a community | Empathy map 5: "wants landing page to look like a community". Affinity: real APSS photos, no AI images |
| Language switcher | Customer check-in, October 7: translation plugin for French, Spanish and Portuguese |
| Neutral look, white page, coloured footer | Customer check-in, October 7: branding direction |

## Home page outline

1. **Top bar:** phone, today's hours, Contact Us, language.
2. **Hero:** a real photo of APSS staff or the building. "Reducing Harms, Improving Lives." Buttons: Get Help, Donate.
3. **Quick actions:** six clickable tiles: Get Tested, Needle Exchange, Report a Needle ↗, Naloxone, Support Groups, Volunteer.
4. **Visit us:** address with a map link, today's hours, and the Thursday testing clinic.
5. **Who we are:** two sentences and a real staff photo, linking to About Us.
6. **Our research:** one featured project card, with "Visit our research site ↗".
7. **News & events:** the latest three items.
8. **Impact numbers:** kept from the current site, which the affinity diagram supports.
9. **Footer.**
